#uni/courses/fluid1 

The continuum principle is a mathematical model to describe [[Fluid]] motion.

It is based on [[Fluid Particle|fluid particles]] at a mathematical point in space with measure 0. A flow domain is then always densely filled with fluid particles. Fluid particle properties are assumed to be described by functions of their $\upxi$ and of time $t$, they can be measured by statistical averaging over the ensemble of molecules which happen to represent the fluid particles at that location and time.

If the continuum principle can be used depends on the [[Knudsen Number]].
