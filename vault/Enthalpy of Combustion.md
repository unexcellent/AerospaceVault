Symbol: $\overline{h}_{C}$
Unit: $\frac{J}{mol}$

For [[Combustion]] processes, the [[Enthalpy]] of reaction is usually referred to as the enthalpy of combustion.

It represents the amount of [[Heat]] released during a steady-flow combustion process when 1 kmol (or 1 kg) of [[Fuel]] is burned completely at a specified [[Temperature]] and [[Pressure]].

![[Pasted image 20250104205113.png|400]]
$$
\overline{h}_{C} = \sum_{p} y_{p} \cdot \overline{h}_{f,p} - \sum_{r} y_{r} \cdot \overline{h}_{f,r}
$$
-> $\overline{h}_{f,P}$: [[Enthalpy of Formation]] of the products
-> $\overline{h}_{f,R}$: [[Enthalpy of Formation]] of the reactants
