In contrast to [[Ideal Gas|ideal gases]], real gases are usually mixtures of more than one type of molecule. These result in intermolecular interactions.

Gas mixtures can be either
- non-reacting
- reacting

# Ideal Gas Mixture

$$
y_{i} = \frac{P_{i}}{P_{m}} = \frac{V_{i}}{V_{m}} = \frac{N_{i}}{N_{m}}
$$
-> $y_{i}$: fraction of gas $i$
-> $P_{i}$: component [[Pressure]]
-> $V_{i}$: component [[Volume]]
-> $N_{i}$: component [[Amount of Substance]]
-> $P_{m}$: mixture [[Pressure]]
-> $V_{m}$: mixture [[Volume]]
-> $N_{m}$: mixture [[Amount of Substance]]

# Real Gas Mixture

- [[Dalton's Law]] and [[Amagat's Law]] apply to real gasses with with reasonable accuracy
- however, the deviations should be taken into account

## Compressibility Factor

$$
Z_{m} = \sum^{}_{i} y_{i} \cdot Z_{i}
$$
-> $Z_{m}$: mixture [[Compressibility Factor]]
-> $y_{i}$: fraction of gas $i$
-> $Z_{i}$: component [[Compressibility Factor]]
