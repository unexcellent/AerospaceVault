#uni/courses/fluid1 

Symbol: $F_{V}$
Unit: $N$

The body force is the [[Mass]]-specific distributed resultant [[Force]] acting on a [[Fluid Particle]] of a fluid volume.
$$
\underline{F}_{V} = \int_{\tilde{V}} \rho \cdot \underline{f} \ dV
$$
-> $V$: [[Volume]]
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $f$: distributed [[Force|forces]]
