The Prandtl tube is a device for measuring flow [[Velocity]] applying the [[Bernoulli's Principle]] to a flow device.

![[Pasted image 20250126164401.png|600]]
$$
q_{1} = \sqrt{2 \frac{\Delta p}{\rho}}
$$
-> $q$: flow [[Velocity]] [[Vector#Euclidian Length|magnitude]]
-> $\Delta p$: [[Pressure]] difference
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
