#uni/courses/thermo2 

Symbol: $P_{i}$
Unit: $Pa$

The partial pressure of a [[Gas Mixture|mixture]] component $i$ is the [[Pressure]] that only that component is asserting.
$$
P_{i} = \frac{n_{i}}{n_{tot}} \cdot P_{tot}
$$
-> $n_{i}$: [[Amount of Substance]] of component
-> $n_{tot}$: [[Amount of Substance]] of mixture
-> $P_{tot}$: [[Pressure]] of mixture
