When a [[Mach Number|supersonic flow]] is turned in the opposite direction due to an angled obstacle, a phenomenon called Prandtl-Meyer expansion waves are formed.
![[Pasted image 20250204102224.png|500]]
As each successive Mach wave turns the flow by an infinitesimal amount, individual expansion wave is nearly isentropic, the flow across the entire expansion fan is also nearly isentropic.
