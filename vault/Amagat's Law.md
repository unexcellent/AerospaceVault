Amagat's law states that the [[Volume]] of a [[Gas Mixture]] is equal to the sum of the volumes each gas would occupy if it existed alone at the mixture [[Temperature]] and [[Pressure]].
$$
V_{m} = \sum^{k}_{i=1} V_{i}(T_{m}, P_{m})
$$
-> $V_{m}$: mixture [[Volume]]
-> $V_{i}$: component [[Volume]]
-> $T_{m}$: mixture [[Temperature]]
-> $P_{m}$: mixture [[Pressure]]

The law holds exact for [[Ideal Gas|ideal gasses]] and approximate for gas mixtures.
