Circular motion is the translation process caused by translation around and axis. The following rules apply
$$
\overrightarrow{v}(t) = \overrightarrow{\omega} \times \overrightarrow{r}
$$
$$
\overrightarrow{a}(t) = \overrightarrow{\alpha} \times \overrightarrow{r} + \overrightarrow{\omega} \times \overrightarrow{v}
$$
-> $r$: [[Position]]
-> $v$: [[Velocity]]
-> $\omega$: [[Angular Velocity]]
-> $a$: [[Acceleration]]
-> $\alpha$: [[Angular Acceleration]]
-> $t$: time

If the angular velocity only exists in the $z$-axis:
$$
\overrightarrow{a}(t) = \overrightarrow{\alpha} \times \overrightarrow{r} - \omega^{2} \cdot \overrightarrow{r}
$$
