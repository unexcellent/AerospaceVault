#uni/courses/thermo2 

A simple rule used to calculate the pseudo-[[Critical Phase Point|critical]] properties of a [[Gas Mixture]].
$$
P'_{cr,m} = \sum_{i} y_{i} \cdot P_{cr,i}
$$
$$
T'_{cr,m} = \sum_{i} y_{i} \cdot T_{cr,i}
$$
-> $P'_{cr,m}$: mixture [[Critical Phase Point|critical]] [[Pressure]]
-> $P_{cr,i}$: component [[Critical Phase Point|critical]] [[Pressure]]
-> $T'_{cr,m}$: mixture [[Critical Phase Point|critical]] [[Temperature]]
-> $T_{cr,i}$: component [[Critical Phase Point|critical]] [[Temperature]]
-> $y_{i}$: fraction of gas $i$
