#uni/courses/thermo2 

Symbol: $K_{P}$

The equilibrium constant of a [[Chemical Reaction]] is the value of its reaction quotient at [[Equilibrium#Chemical Equilibrium|chemical equilibrium]].
$$
\ce{
\upsilon_{A} A + \upsilon_{B} B
<=>
\upsilon_{C} C + \upsilon_{D} D
}
$$
-> $\upsilon_{i}$: stoichiometric coefficient of compound $i$

# Properties

- $K_{P}$ of a reaction only depends on [[Temperature]]
- the $K_{P}$ of the reverse reaction is $\frac{1}{K_{P}}$
- reactions with larger $K_{P}$ are more complete

# Expressions

## Partial Pressure

$$
K_{P} = \frac{P_{C}^{\upsilon_{C}} \cdot P_{D}^{\upsilon_{D}}}{P_{A}^{\upsilon_{A}} \cdot P_{B}^{\upsilon_{B}}}
$$
-> $P_{i}$: partial [[Pressure]] of compound $i$
-> $\upsilon_{i}$: stoichiometric coefficient of compound $i$

## Standard Gibbs Function

$$
K_{P} = e^{- \frac{\Delta G^{*}(T)}{R_{u} \cdot T}}
$$
-> $\Delta G^{*}(T)$: standard [[Gibbs Energy|Gibbs]] function change
-> $R_{u}$: [[Universal Gas Constant]]
-> $T$: [[Temperature]]

## Equilibrium Composition

$$
K_{P} = \frac{N_{C}^{\upsilon_{C}} \cdot N_{D}^{\upsilon_{D}}}{N_{A}^{\upsilon_{A}} \cdot N_{B}^{\upsilon_{B}}} \cdot \left( \frac{P}{N_{total}} \right)^{\Delta \upsilon}
\quad \text{with} \quad
\Delta \upsilon = \upsilon_{C} + \upsilon_{D} - \upsilon_{A} - \upsilon_{B}
$$
-> $N_{i}$: [[Amount of Substance]] of compound $i$
-> $P$: total [[Pressure]]
-> $N_{total}$: total [[Amount of Substance]]
