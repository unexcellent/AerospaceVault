#uni/courses/thermo1 

Symbol: $M$
Unit: $\frac{g}{mol}$

Molar mass is the ratio between the [[Mass]] and the [[Amount of Substance]] and is a constant for any substance.
$$
M = \frac{m}{n}
$$
-> $m$: [[Mass]]
-> $n$: [[Amount of Substance]]

# Common Substances

| Substance      | Molar Mass in $kg/mol$ | Molar Mass in $g/mol$ |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------- |
| Air            | $0.02896$              | $28.84$               |
| Argon          | $0.04$                 | $40.0$                |
| Carbon Dioxide | $0.044$                | $44.0$                |
| Helium         | $0.004$                | $4.0$                 |
| Hydrogen       | $0.002016$             | $2.016$               |
| Nitrogen       | $0.028$                | $28.0$                |
| Oxygen         | $0.032$                | $32.0$                |
| Water          | $0.018$                | $18.0$                |

# Average Molar Mass

The average molar mass of a [[Gas Mixture]] is simply the the ratio of the total [[Mass]] to the total [[Amount of Substance]]
$$
M_{m} = \frac{m_{m}}{N_{m}}
$$
