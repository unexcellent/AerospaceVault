The Venturi tube is a device for measuring flow [[Velocity]] by changing the cross section of a tube and measuring the [[Pressure]] difference. It is based on [[Bernoulli's Principle]].
![[Pasted image 20250126163659.png|600]]
$$
q_{1} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot \Delta p}{\rho} \left( 1 - \left( \frac{A_{1}}{A_{2}} \right)^{2} \right)^{-1}}
$$
-> $q$: flow [[Velocity]] [[Vector#Euclidian Length|magnitude]]
-> $\Delta p$: [[Pressure]] difference
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $A$: cross sectional area

The measurement is only accurate however if the inflow is in the axial direction of the tube.
