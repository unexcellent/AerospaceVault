Oblique shock is a [[Shock Wave]] that is inclined with respect to the direction of incoming air. This results in a deflection by a deflection angle $\theta$. 
![[Pasted image 20250202161811.png]]
![[Pasted image 20250202161911.png|500]]

$$
\tan \theta = \frac{2 \cdot \cot(\beta) \cdot (Ma_{1}^{2} \cdot \sin^{2}(\beta)-1)}{Ma_{1}^{2} \cdot (k + \cos(2 \beta) + 2)}
$$
-> $\beta$: shock wave angle
-> $Ma$: [[Mach Number]]
-> $k$: [[Isentropic Exponent]]
