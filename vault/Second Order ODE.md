An [[Ordinary Differential Equation]] is considered of second order if two [[Derivative|derivatives]] are included in the equation.

The initial value problem is then defined as:
$$
y''(x) + p(x) \cdot y'(x) + q(x) \cdot y(x) = r(x)
$$
$$
y(x_{0}) = y_{0}
$$
$$
y'(x_{0}) = y'_{0}
$$
