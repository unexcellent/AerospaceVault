# Basic Considerations

![[Pasted image 20250202150938.png|500]]

Mass conservation:
$$
\rho_{1} \cdot V_{1} = \rho_{2} \cdot V_{2}
$$
Energy conservation:
$$
h_{1} + \frac{V_{1}^{2}}{2} = h_{2} + \frac{V_{2}^{2}}{2} 
$$
Momentum equation:
$$
A \cdot (P_{1}-P_{2}) = \dot{m} \cdot (V_{1} - V_{2})
$$
State of equation:
$$
P = \rho \cdot R \cdot T
$$
