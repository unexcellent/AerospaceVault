Combustion describes an exothermic [[Chemical Reaction]] during which a [[Fuel]] is oxidized.

# Completeness

A combustion process is called **complete** if all the combustible components of a fuel are burned to completion.

# Stoichiometricity

An ideal combustion process during which a fuel is
burned completely with theoretical air is called the stoichiometric or theoretical combustion of that fuel.
