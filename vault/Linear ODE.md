A linear ODE is an [[Ordinary Differential Equation]] where the $y$ component only exists in linear form.
$$
p_{0}(x) \cdot y + p_{1}(x) \cdot y' + \dots + p_{n}(x) \cdot y^{(n)} = r(x) 
$$

If $b(x) \equiv 0$, then the equation is called homogeneous, if not it is called inhomogeneous.
