Symbol: $c$
Unit: $\frac{m}{s}$

The speed of sound is the [[Velocity]] at which a infinitesimally small [[Pressure]] wave travels through a medium.
$$
c = \sqrt{k \cdot R \cdot T}
$$
-> $k$: [[Isentropic Exponent]]
-> $R$: [[Universal Gas Constant]]
-> $T$: [[Temperature]]
