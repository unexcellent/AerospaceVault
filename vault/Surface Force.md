#uni/courses/fluid1 

Symbol: $F_{S}$
Unit: $N$

Surface forces are the distributed [[Force|forces]] that act on [[Fluid Particle|fluid particles]] surfaces.
$$
\underline{F}_{S} = \int_{S} \underline{\underline{\sigma}}^{T} \cdot \underline{n} \ dS = \int_{S} (-p \cdot \underline{\underline{I}} + \underline{\underline{\tau}}) \cdot \underline{n} \ dS
$$
-> $S$: [[Surface Integral]]
-> $\sigma$: [[Stress]]
-> $T$: [[Transposition of a Matrix]]
-> $n$: normal vector
-> $p$: [[Pressure]]
-> $I$: [[Matrix#Identity Matrix|identity matrix]]
-> $\tau$: [[Stress#Tensor Representation|stress tensor]]
