Symbol: $\phi$
Unit: $1$

Relative humidity describes the ratio between the [[Mass]] of water in a gas compared to the maximum [[Mass]] of water the gas can hold at that [[Temperature]].
$$
\phi = \frac{P_{v}}{P_{g}} = \frac{\omega P}{(0.622 + \omega)P_{g}}
$$
-> $P_{v}$: [[Pressure]] of the water vapor
-> $P_{g}$: [[Pressure]] of the gas
-> $\omega$: [[Specific Humidity]]
-> $P$: [[Pressure]] of the mixture
