#uni/courses/thermo2 

Symbol: $P_{0}$
Unit: $Pa$

The stagnation pressure is the [[Pressure]] of a [[Fluid]] at the [[Stagnation Point]] of a steady-flow process.
$$
P_{0} = P \cdot \left( \frac{T_{0}}{T} \right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma - 1}}
$$
-> $P$: [[Pressure]] of [[Fluid]]
-> $T_{0}$: [[Stagnation Temperature]]
-> $T$: [[Temperature]] of [[Fluid]]
-> $\gamma$: [[Isentropic Exponent]]

# For Isentropic Nozzle Flow

$$
P_{0} = P_{1} \cdot \left( 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2} Ma_{1}^{2} \right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}
$$

-> $P_{1}$: [[Pressure]] at nozzle entrance
-> $\gamma$: [[Isentropic Exponent]]
-> $Ma_1$: [[Mach Number]] at nozzle entrance

In practice the values for $\frac{P_{0}}{P_{1}}$ can often be read from the tables.
