Symbol: $\omega$
Unit: $1$

Specific humidity describes the ratio between the [[Mass]] of water compared to the [[Mass]] of dry gas.
$$
\omega = \frac{m_{v}}{m_{g}}
$$
-> $m_{v}$: [[Mass]] of the water vapor
-> $m_{v}$: [[Mass]] of the dry gas

# For Air

$$
\omega = 0.622 \cdot \frac{P_{v}}{P-P_{v}}
$$
-> $P_{v}$: [[Pressure]] of the water vapor
-> $P$: [[Pressure]] of the mixture
