#uni/courses/fluid1 

Mass conservation describes the constant nature of [[Mass]] in a unit of fluid volume.
$$
0 = \frac{dm}{dt} 
= \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \text{div}(\rho \cdot \underline{u}) 
= \int_{V} \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} dV + \int_{S} \rho \cdot u_{j} \cdot n_{j} \ dS
$$
-> $m$: [[Mass]]
-> $t$: time
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $u$: [[Velocity]]
-> $V$: [[Volume]]
-> $S$: [[Surface Integral]]
-> $n$: normal vector to $u$
