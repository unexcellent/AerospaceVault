Linear motion is the translation process without change in direction. The following rules apply
$$
\overrightarrow{v}(t) = \frac{d\overrightarrow{r}(t)}{dt}
$$
$$
\overrightarrow{a}(t) = \frac{d\overrightarrow{v}(t)}{dt}
$$
-> $r$: [[Position]]
-> $v$: [[Velocity]]
-> $a$: [[Acceleration]]
-> $t$: time
