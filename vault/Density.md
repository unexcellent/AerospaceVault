Symbol: $\rho$
Unit: $\frac{kg}{m^{3}}$

The density of a [[Material]] describes [[Mass]] per unit [[Volume]].

For [[Ideal Gas|ideal gases]] the density is calculated as
$$
\rho = \frac{R \cdot T}{p}
$$
-> $R$: [[Universal Gas Constant]]
-> $T$: [[Temperature]]
-> $p$: [[Pressure]]
