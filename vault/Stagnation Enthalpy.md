#uni/courses/thermo2 

Symbol: $h_{0}$
Unit: $\frac{J}{kg}$

The stagnation enthalpy is the [[Enthalpy]] of a [[Fluid]] when it is [[Adiabatic Process|adiabatically]] brought to rest on the [[Stagnation Point]].

$$
h_{0} = h + \frac{v^{2}}{2}
$$
-> $h$: specific [[Enthalpy]]
-> $v$: fluid [[Velocity]] at point of interest

The stagnation enthalpy does not depend of the amount of [[Entropy]] generated during the process.
