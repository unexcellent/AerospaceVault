The Wronski determinant is the [[Determinant of a Matrix|determinant]] formed by $n$ [[Function|functions]] and their $(n-1)$ [[Derivative|derivates]].
$$
W(f_{1}, \dots, f_{n})(x) = \det \begin{pmatrix}
f_{1}(x) & f_{2}(x) & \cdots &  f_{n}(x) \\ 
f'_{1}(x) & f'_{2}(x) & \cdots &  f'_{n}(x) \\ 
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 
f^{(n-1)}_{1}(x) & f^{(n-1)}_{2}(x) & \cdots &  f^{(n-1)}_{n}(x) \\ 
\end{pmatrix}
$$
