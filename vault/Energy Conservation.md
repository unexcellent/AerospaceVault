#uni/courses/fluid1 

Energy conservation describes the constant nature of the energy of a [[Fluid Particle]].
$$
\int_V \frac{\partial E}{\partial t} dV 
+ \int_{S} E \cdot u_{j} \cdot n_{j} \ dS 
= - \int_{S} p \cdot u_{j} \cdot n_{j} \ dS
+ \int_{S} \tau_{ij} \cdot u_{j} \cdot n_{j} \ dS
+ \int_{V} \rho \cdot f_{j} \cdot u_{j} \ dV
- \int_{S} q_{j} \cdot n_{j} \ dS
$$
-> $E$: energy
-> $t$: time
-> $V$: [[Volume]]
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $u$: [[Velocity]]
-> $S$: surface
-> $n$: normal vector
-> $p$: [[Pressure]]
-> $\tau$: [[Stress#Tensor Representation|stress tensor]]
-> $f$: distributed [[Force]]
-> $q$: [[Heat]]
