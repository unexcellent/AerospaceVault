The Bernoulli principle gives a relationship between the physical quantities of [[Pressure]] and [[Velocity|flow velocity]] along a [[Streamline]] $S$.
![[Pasted image 20250126160649.png|500]]
![[Pasted image 20250126161451.png|300]]

This would allow the calculation of lift along an airfoil.

The general form of the Bernoulli equation is defined as
$$
0 = \int_{S_{1}}^{S_{2}} \frac{\partial q}{\partial t} ds + \left[ \frac{q^{2}}{2} \right]_{S_{1}}^{S_{2}} + \int_{S_{1}}^{S_{2}} \frac{1}{\rho} dp + \left[ G \right]_{S_{1}}^{S_{2}}
$$
-> $S,s$: [[Streamline]]
-> $q$: [[Vector#Euclidian Length|magnitude]] of [[Velocity]] vector
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $p$: [[Pressure]]
-> $G$: [[Force]] [[Potential Energy|potential]] $f=-\nabla G$
