Symbol: $P_{b}$
Unit: $Pa$

Back pressure is the [[Pressure]] created by resistance of [[Fluid]] flow through a pipe or nozzle.

# For Converging-Diverging Nozzles

| Flow regime                       | Criterion                                                                                                   | Features                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No flow                           | $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}}=1$                                                                                     | [[Stagnation Pressure]] is equal to static pressure throughout the nozzle                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Isentropic subsonic throughout    | $\left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sub} < \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} < 1$                                        | Everywhere is subsonic. [[Velocity]] increase in converging and decrease in diverging. $V_{max}$ at the throat                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Choked isentropic subsonic flow   | $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} = \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sub}$                                            | Only sonic at the throat, the diverging section is chocked ($\frac{P*}{P_{}0}=0.528$ for $k=1.4$) and does not change with further decrease of $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}}$                                                                                                                                 |
| Choked isentropic supersonic flow | $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} = \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sup}$                                            | Subsonic in converging, sonic at the throat and supersonic in diverging. Isentropic flow throughout                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Normal shock inside nozzle        | $\left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{NSE} < \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} < \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sub}$ | $P_{b}$ is too low to have fully subsonic flow in the diverging section. Normal shock forms and the flow post-shock has to be subsonic to increase static pressure to $P_b$. Shock location depends on $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}}$                                                                         |
| Normal shock at the nozzle exit   | $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} = \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{NSE}$                                            | Supersonic flow through the entire diverging session of the nozzle at the exit, normal shock forms to increase $P_{e}$ to $P_{b}$. It is the last $P_{b}$ that will cause changes inside the nozzle. As further decrease of $P_{b}$, changes occur outside the nozzle.                               |
| Overexpanded flow                 | $\left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sub} < \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} < \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{NSE}$ | Nozzle has expanded the flow too much, $P_{e}$ is too low comparing to $P_{b}$, Normal shock at the exit is too strong. Pressure is increased through weak oblique shocks. Actually complex oblique shocks intervening with expansion fans, to equalize the flow to back pressure outside the nozzle |
| Under expanded flow               | $\frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} = \left( \frac{P_{b}}{P_{0}} \right)_{sub}$                                            | Nozzle has not expanded the flow enough, $P_{e}$ is higher than $P_{b}$, Pressure is decreased through expansion fans. Actually alternating EF and oblique shocks form to equalize the flow to back pressure outside the nozzle.                                                                     |
