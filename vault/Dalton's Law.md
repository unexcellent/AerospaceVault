Dalton's states that the [[Pressure]] of a [[Gas Mixture]] is equal to the sum of the pressures each gas would exert if it existed alone at the mixture [[Temperature]] and [[Volume]].
$$
P_{m} = \sum^{k}_{i=1} P_{i}(T_{m}, V_{m})
$$
-> $P_{m}$: mixture [[Pressure]]
-> $P_{i}$: component [[Pressure]]
-> $T_{m}$: mixture [[Temperature]]
-> $V_{m}$: mixture [[Volume]]
