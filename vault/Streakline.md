The streakline is the connecting line of all [[Fluid Particle]] at a time $t$ that in their past have traversed through the same [[Position|location]] $\overline{\underline{x}}$.
![[Pasted image 20250120191004.png|500]]

# Examples

- smoke emerging from a chimney: the visible plume is generated by soot particles that in the past have emerged from the chimney opening
- “smoke” (actually oil droplets) issued from a smoke-generator tube in a wind tunnel
