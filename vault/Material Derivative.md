#uni/courses/fluid1 

The material derivative describes the [[Derivative|rate of change]] of some physical quantity of a material element that is subjected to a [[Velocity]] [[Vector Field|field]].
$$
\frac{D\Psi}{Dt} = \underbrace{\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}}_{\text{local}} + \underbrace{\underline{u} \cdot \nabla \Psi}_\text{convective} = \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} + \underline{u} \cdot e_{1} \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x_{1}} + \underline{u} \cdot e_{2} \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x_{2}} + \underline{u} \cdot e_{3} \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x_{3}}
$$
-> $\Psi$: physical quantity
-> $t$: time
-> $\underline{u}$: [[Velocity]]
-> $\nabla \Psi$: [[Partial Derivative#Gradient|gradient]] of the physical quantity
-> $e_{i}$: canonical basis
