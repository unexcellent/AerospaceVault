During [[Fluid]] flow through devices with changing cross-sectional areas, energy is exchanged in an approximately [[Isentropic Process]].
![[Pasted image 20250118160833.png|350]]

The following equations hold true:
$$
\dot{m} = const.
$$

$$
\frac{d\rho}{\rho} + \frac{dA}{A} + \frac{dv}{v} = 0
$$
$$
h_{1} + \frac{v_{1}^{2}}{2} = h_{2} + \frac{v_{2}^{2}}{2}
$$
$$
\frac{dP}{\rho} + v \ dv = 0
$$
$$
\frac{dA}{A} = \frac{dP}{\rho} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{v^{2}} - \frac{d\rho}{dP} \right) = \frac{dP}{\rho \cdot v^{2}} \cdot (1 - Ma^{2}) = - \frac{dv}{v} \cdot (1 - Ma^{2})
$$

-> $\dot{m}$: [[Mass]] flow rate
-> $\rho$: [[Density]]
-> $A$: area
-> $v$: [[Velocity]]
-> $h$: [[Enthalpy]]
-> $P$: [[Pressure]]
-> $Ma$: [[Mach Number]]
