#uni/courses/mech3 

Symbol: $v_{p}$
Unit: $\frac{m}{s}$

The path speed is the [[Velocity]] of an object while rotating around an axis.
![[Angular Velocity 2024-11-10 16.25.52.excalidraw]]
$$
\overrightarrow{v}_{p}(t) = \overrightarrow{\omega} \times \overrightarrow{r}_{p}(t)
$$
-> $\omega$: [[Angular Velocity]]
-> $r$: [[Rotation Angle]]
