Symbol: $\omega$
Unit: $\frac{rad}{s}$

Angular velocity describes the [[Velocity|speed]] of an object rotating around an axis. It is the first [[Derivative]] of [[Rotation Angle]].
![[Angular Velocity 2024-11-10 16.25.52.excalidraw]]

In 3D it is a [[Vector]] parallel to the axis of rotation and orthogonal of the rotation plane.
