Symbol: $\phi$
Unit: $1$

The equivalence ratio is the ratio between the actual [[Fuel]]-air ratio compared to the [[Combustion#Stoichiometricity|stoichiometric]] fuel-air value during [[Combustion]].
- $\phi = 1$: stoichiometric combustion
- $\phi > 1$: more fuel than theoretical value (less oxygen): rich combustion
	-  e.g., $\phi = 1.2$ means 16.67% deficiency of air.
- $\phi < 1$: less fuel than theoretical value (more oxygen): lean combustion
	- e.g., $\phi = 0.8$ means 25% excess of air.
