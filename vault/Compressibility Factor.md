#uni/courses/thermo1 

Symbol: $Z$
Unit: $1$

The compressibility factor states the deviation of real [[States of Matter#Gas|gas]] from [[Ideal Gas]].
- $Z < 1$: Real gas is more dense than ideal gas
- $Z > 1$: Real gas is less dense than ideal gas
