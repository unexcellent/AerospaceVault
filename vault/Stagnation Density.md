#uni/courses/thermo2 

Symbol: $\rho_{0}$
Unit: $\frac{kg}{m^{3}}$

The stagnation density is the [[Density]] of a [[Fluid]] at the [[Stagnation Point]] of a steady-flow process.
$$
\rho_{0} = \rho \cdot \left( \frac{T_{0}}{T} \right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma - 1}}
$$
-> $\rho$: density of [[Fluid]]
-> $T_{0}$: [[Stagnation Temperature]]
-> $T$: [[Temperature]] of [[Fluid]]
-> $\gamma$: [[Isentropic Exponent]]
