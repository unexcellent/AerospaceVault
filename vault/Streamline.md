A streamline is an [[Integration|integral]] [[Curve]] of the instantaneous [[Velocity]] [[Vector Field|field]]. At any location they are tangential with respect to the instantaneous velocity field.
![[Pasted image 20250120190128.png|500]]

The definition equation of streamlines is
$$
\frac{dx_{S1}}{u_{1}} = \frac{dx_{S2}}{u_{2}} = \frac{dx_{S3}}{u_{3}}
$$
-> $x_{Si}$: [[Position]] on axis $i$
-> $u_{i}$: [[Velocity]] on axis $i$
