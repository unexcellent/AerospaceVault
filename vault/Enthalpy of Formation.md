Symbol: $\overline{h}_{f}^{\circ}$
Unit: $\frac{J}{mol}$

Enthalpy of formation can be viewed as the [[Enthalpy]] of a substance at a specified state due to its [[Chemical Energy|chemical composition]].

For any compound, the enthalpy of formation is calculated from its basic elements in a steady flow process or extracted from tables.
